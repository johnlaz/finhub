From export to review in three steps

No accounting software connection and no account to create.

1

Ingest

Zip your A/R and A/P aging, balance sheet, P&L and trial balance exports and load the ZIP. File names are matched loosely.

2

Organize

FinHub detects the columns, even across QuickBooks and Odoo layouts, and sorts everything into one document library.

3

Analyze

Read the executive summary, drill into each report, and add AI commentary with your own Groq key.
